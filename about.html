The Difference

What Is Mental Wellness Coaching?

There's a gap between functioning and performing. Mental wellness coaching is about closing it.

Most high achievers are already mentally healthy. You're not in crisis — you're getting it done, hitting the numbers, showing up. But "getting it done" and "performing at your best" are two very different things.

Mental wellness coaching is for the people who are doing well and know they could be doing remarkably better. It's optimization, not repair. We take a mind that already works and tune it — so you think clearer, recover faster, stay consistent, and stop leaving performance on the table.

This is for healthy, driven people who want to be mentally optimized — not for people in crisis or in need of treatment.

Functioning vs. Performing

Functioning is keeping all the plates spinning. Performing is doing it with focus, energy, and confidence to spare — and still having something left for the people and life you're working so hard for. Coaching is how you get from one to the other.

This Is Coaching, Not Therapy

Mental wellness coaching is forward-focused performance work for healthy, high-functioning people who want to optimize. It is not therapy, counseling, or treatment for mental health conditions, and it's not a substitute for care from a licensed professional. If you're navigating a mental health crisis or condition, please reach out to a qualified provider — and Shawn is always glad to work alongside that care once you're well supported.
