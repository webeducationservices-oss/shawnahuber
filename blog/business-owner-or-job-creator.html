Business Owner Or Job Creator?

Shawn Huber · September 5, 2022

If you are not in your business, will it function? Will the lights stay on? Will the bills get paid? If you are not in your business, who will have the knowledge and experience to make decisions? Who will be able to take care of customers, employees and vendors? If you don't know how to do something, how can you train someone else?

The first thing to do is to make sure that your business is doing what you want it to do. This can be hard as there are so many aspects to a business, but if you don't know what you want, then how can you achieve it? Do you have a plan for the future? Do you know where your business is going? What are your goals? Where do they fit into your life goals? What kind of lifestyle do you want? Are these in line with the direction of the business?

If they aren't then maybe it's time to adjust things.

Maybe this means hiring someone else or outsourcing some of the workload, maybe it means becoming more involved or maybe it means selling and moving on to something else. You should never stay in a job or a career just because it pays well, because if that's all that matters then all we need are robots!

Business owners and job creators are not the same.

If you are a business owner, your primary focus is on the bottom line — making money. If you are a job creator, your primary focus is on the people you employ — providing them with opportunities to succeed. As a business owner, you can make money by selling your business or by selling products or services. But if you are not in your business will it function?

Being a job creator

As a job creator, you can make money by growing your business and hiring more people to help build it. This will allow you to expand into new markets, increase profits and provide more jobs for others who need them. As a job creator, you have an obligation to share what you know with others so they can grow as well! Spread your knowledge by teaching others in your business or even start a school or training center that teaches new employees how to do their jobs well. So what do you want to be? Do you want to be confused, stressed out with "Trying" to make it, and when you try to sell your business it is worthless? Or are you interested in having a great impact on your customers and employees? Let me help you take a look at yourself first and then what you want your business to do for you.
