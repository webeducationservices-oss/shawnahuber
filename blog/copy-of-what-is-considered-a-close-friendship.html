Ways To Reduce Holiday Stress

Shawn Huber · December 02, 2022

Getting stressed over the holidays

The holidays are a time for family, friends and festivities. Unfortunately, they can also be a time for stress. From shopping for gifts to planning parties, preparing meals and traveling, the stress can pile up quickly.

Eating healthy and still enjoying holiday parties

One way to reduce holiday stress is to plan ahead and make sure you have healthy food options at your holiday parties. This way, you won't feel tempted to eat unhealthy foods from the buffet table or snack table. If you don't want to bring your own food or there's no room in your car, consider asking someone else to bring something healthy so there are some good choices available.

Learn how with the 2 Day Mindset Challenge

The 2 Day Mindset Challenge will help you shift your mindset so that you can enjoy the holidays without getting overwhelmed by all the activities and expectations surrounding them. It's easy to get caught up in worry about what others think of us or whether we have enough money or time or whatever other things we tell ourselves when we're under stress or feeling pressured by our schedules — but if we could just stop doing that for two days (even though it might take longer), then maybe we would really enjoy the holidays and be better prepared for the New Year.
