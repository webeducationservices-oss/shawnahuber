Feeling Excited, Frustrated, Disappointed, Out Of Balance—Take Control Before The New Year With This

Shawn Huber · November 11, 2022

If you're anything like me, you are ready for the new year. It's so exciting to think about all the possibilities that will be open to us in 2023. But before we get too far ahead of ourselves, there's something we need to do: prepare our mindsets for success in the new year.

It's easy to get caught up in the excitement of a fresh start and just jump right into it, but if your mind isn't ready for change, then what's the point? You'll just end up feeling frustrated and disappointed when things don't go the way you hoped they would.

If your mind isn't ready for change, you'll end up feeling frustrated and disappointed when things don't go the way you hoped. — Mental Preparation Truth

Why Taking Control Before January Matters

That's why it's important to take a mindset challenge before the new year begins. This is a great way to make sure that your brain is ready to handle whatever comes its way—and if it isn't, then now is the time to make some changes so that next year will be even better than this one!

Coach's Insight

Most people wait until January 1st to start working on their mindset. The winners start preparing their mental foundation weeks earlier.

What You'll Learn in the 5-Day Challenge

Our 5-Day Mindset Awareness Challenge is designed to set your new year up for success by building the mental foundation you need. Here's what you'll master:

1

Becoming Aware of Your Influences

Learn to identify and evaluate the people, environments, and inputs that shape your thinking patterns daily.

2

Building a Foundation Through Positive Thought and Action

Develop the mental habits and behavioral patterns that create momentum and sustained progress.

3

Learning How to Identify Personality Traits

Understand yourself and others better to improve relationships and communication effectiveness.

4

Becoming a Better Communicator

Master the skills needed to express your vision and connect with others who can help you achieve your goals.

5

Learning from Others Who Have Been Through the Program

Gain insights and strategies from people who have successfully transformed their mindset and results.

Your Last Chance Before the New Year

This is the last 5-day challenge before the new year. There are limited spots available because we offer this introductory course for free. The time to prepare your mind for success is now, not after you've already struggled through the first few weeks of January.

Limited Time Opportunity

If you can't make the November challenge, there is a 2-day intensive course in December. Message us for details.

Key Takeaways

  • Success in the new year requires mental preparation before January 1st
  • Mindset awareness is the foundation of lasting change and achievement
  • Understanding your influences and communication patterns accelerates progress
  • Taking action now prevents frustration and disappointment later
