Get Your Vibe Back With This Mental Mechanic Tool, The Box Breath

Shawn Huber · May 12, 2022

The Box Breath is a breathing technique that helps you to reset your thoughts and emotions. It's a fast way to get back into a positive state of mind and relax your body.

This powerful tool offers fast reset from negative to positive thought and can help release negative tension in seconds. By using this technique, you can stay focused and present in any situation, no matter what happens around you.

Why Box Breathing Works for High Performers

The Box Breath is perfect for on-the-go business owners who need to be positive in all situations. This technique helps you transition from one situation to another without carrying over negative energy, frustration, and stress into the next important activity.

It's also invaluable for helping you transition from business owner mode to your personal self, creating clear mental boundaries between your professional and personal life.

Coach's Insight

The Box Breath creates an instant mental reset, allowing you to show up fully present for whatever comes next in your day.

How to Practice Box Breathing

Here's the step-by-step process to master this mental mechanic tool:

1

Set Up Your Space

Find a comfortable place to sit or lay down. Limit distractions for 5 minutes and put that phone on hold.

2

Ground Yourself

Close your eyes and take a big deep breath in and release slowly. Allow yourself to be present in the moment.

3

Begin the Box Pattern

Here's where the magic starts. Begin to take a breath for 4 seconds, hold for 2, then release for 5 seconds, and finally hold for 2 seconds before your next breath.

4

Build Your Practice

Repeat this pattern several times before adding time to the process. As you advance, add a second to the inhale and a second to the exhale. Always keep the exhale 1 second longer than the inhale.

5

Customize Your Duration

You can do this for 3 to 10 minutes or more depending on the time you have. Your increase in time is up to your comfort level.

The Immediate Benefits You'll Experience

What you may notice after doing this Box Breath technique:

  • Lower heart rate and reduced blood pressure
  • Decreased anger and frustration
  • Enhanced relaxation and mental clarity
  • Improved focus for your next activity
The Box Breath creates an instant bridge from stress to success, allowing you to reset your mental state in minutes. — Mental Performance Tool

Key Takeaways

  • Box breathing provides instant mental and emotional reset in just minutes
  • Perfect for busy professionals transitioning between high-stakes situations
  • Simple 4-2-5-2 pattern creates immediate physiological benefits
  • Regular practice builds your mental resilience and emotional control
