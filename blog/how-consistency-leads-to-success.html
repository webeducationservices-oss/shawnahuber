How Consistency Leads To Success

Shawn Huber · October 26, 2022

Consistency is one of the most important things for success. The reason why it's so important is because it allows you to build momentum and achieve your goals faster.

Focus on small tasks for consistency & success

The key to being consistent is to focus on a small task in the present moment and maintain your vision of the future. If you're not focused, committed, or disciplined enough to stick with something short term to get long-term results, then you need to look at yourself and see how consistent you are with other areas of your life.

Being consistent in one area of your life will help build momentum and confidence that will lead to other areas of your life as well. For example, if you're consistently going to the gym then it will increase your overall health which will make you feel better about yourself in general. This can lead to more success in other aspects of your life as well.

Perfection is the enemy of consistency & success

Being consistent doesn't mean doing everything perfect every time either because mistakes will happen along the way but it does mean correcting those mistakes so that they don't happen again. Patience means waiting for the right time and place for things to happen. For example, if you want something but it isn’t ready yet, then it will be worth waiting for it. Patience also means being patient with others who are going through their own struggles. Value means having faith and trust in yourself and your abilities so that when things don't go according to plan or someone you still see how important the goal is and you make adjustments along the way for a better result Belief means having faith that what you want is possible even when things look bleak; believing in yourself and knowing that you deserve better than what you have now; believing that your dreams are possible because others have achieved similar dreams before you did; believing that there are people out there who want to help you achieve your goals; believing in God/religion/spirituality etc… To me these are the things that really move the needle forward in all areas of your life. Keep at it and it will happen. Trust the process.
