How Do You Know If You Are Valuable

Shawn Huber · September 20, 2022

The question of your own value isn't as simple as checking your bank balance or counting your work hours. True self-worth runs deeper — it's about recognizing yourself as fundamentally deserving of respect, success, and happiness. Yet many high achievers struggle with this internal recognition, even when external validation surrounds them.

We all carry some sense of our worth, but accessing and expressing it authentically can be challenging. Understanding your values and how they shape your thoughts, actions, and feelings is the foundation of genuine self-confidence and sustained performance.

The Compliment Deflection Pattern

You know the scenario. You've crushed a major goal or delivered exceptional results, and someone acknowledges your achievement. Your immediate response? Brush it off. Deflect the praise back to them. Maybe you even feel uncomfortable or embarrassed by the recognition.

This automatic deflection happens because deep down, you don't believe you deserve the admiration. It's a protective mechanism that actually undermines your ability to internalize success and build lasting confidence.

Coach's Insight

Every time you deflect genuine praise, you're training your brain to reject evidence of your competence. This creates a cycle where external success never translates to internal confidence.

Why Self-Worth Affects Everything

Your sense of personal value operates on both conscious and subconscious levels, influencing every decision you make. When you truly understand your worth, you make choices that align with your potential rather than settling for what feels "safe" or "realistic."

This internal foundation determines whether you'll pursue that promotion, start that business, or invest in the relationships and opportunities that can transform your life. Your self-worth is the operating system that runs all your other mental programs.

Your self-worth is the operating system that runs all your other mental programs. — Core Performance Principle

Building Authentic Self-Recognition

Developing genuine self-worth isn't about positive affirmations or surface-level confidence tricks. It requires honest assessment of your values, consistent action aligned with those values, and the willingness to acknowledge your growth and contributions.

Start by identifying what you actually stand for — not what you think you should stand for, but what drives your best decisions and actions. When your behavior consistently reflects these core values, your sense of worth becomes unshakeable because it's based on integrity, not external validation.

Key Takeaways

  • Self-worth goes deeper than external markers of success
  • Deflecting compliments trains your brain to reject evidence of your competence
  • Your sense of personal value influences every major decision you make
  • True confidence comes from values-based action, not external validation
