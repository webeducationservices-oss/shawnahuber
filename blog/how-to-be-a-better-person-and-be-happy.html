How To Be A Better Person And Be Happy

Shawn Huber · August 30, 2022

The world is a tough place to live in. It's filled with stress, fear, and worry. Many people are living unhappy lives simply because they've forgotten how to be happy.

Being a better person isn't about snapping your fingers and suddenly becoming more patient, kind, or confident. But there are proven, practical steps you can take that will make you feel better and make lasting change easier to achieve.

Exercise Your Way to Better Mental Health

You hear it all the time, but exercise truly makes you feel better. It's not just fitness advice—it's mental health medicine.

Regular exercise boosts endorphins, improves your sleep quality, and directly impacts your stress levels and anxiety. Research consistently shows that physical activity helps combat depression and other mood disorders by changing your brain chemistry in real time.

Coach's Insight

You don't need to become a gym warrior overnight. Even 20 minutes of daily movement creates measurable improvements in mood and mental clarity.

Take Simple Small Steps for Lasting Improvement

If all you do is wish for something big—like a million dollars or a house on a tropical island—it might take forever before that wish comes true. But when you take small, consistent steps each day toward achieving those goals, they will happen much sooner than expected.

Every day when I wake up, I write down three things I am grateful for and then plan my day accordingly. As I go through my day, I try to keep this gratitude in mind so that I'm sending out positive energy at all times.

Small, consistent daily actions compound into massive life changes. — The Power of Incremental Progress

This isn't just feel-good advice. It's about rewiring your brain's default patterns through consistent, positive micro-habits that build momentum over time.

Recharge Daily to Give Your Best

If you're anything like most high achievers, there are times when you feel like everything is getting on top of you. But taking intentional time out for yourself every day can make all those overwhelming tasks seem much more manageable.

We all need to recharge our mental and emotional batteries, but many of us don't know how or when to do it effectively.

How to Recharge the Right Way

Take time out every day where no phone or computer is around so that you can rest your mind and body for a few minutes before getting back into work mode. You'll be surprised how much more productive and focused you become when you make this a non-negotiable habit.

Key Point

Recharging isn't selfish—it's strategic. When you're operating from a place of mental clarity and emotional balance, everyone around you benefits.

Key Takeaways

  • Exercise is medicine for your mind—even 20 minutes daily creates measurable mood improvements
  • Small, consistent daily actions compound into life-changing results over time
  • Daily recharge time without technology is essential for peak performance and well-being
  • Gratitude practice rewires your brain for positive thinking patterns
