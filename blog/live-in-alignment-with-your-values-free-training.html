Live in Alignment With Your Values: Free Training

Shawn Huber · March 31, 2026

How often do you catch yourself saying one thing and doing another? We all do it. We say we value "family time," but then spend evenings glued to our screens, answering emails. We claim to prioritize "health," but consistently choose convenience foods over nourishing meals.

The truth is, most people aren't consciously choosing to live this way. They drift, not because they're lazy or lack willpower, but because they haven't clearly defined what truly matters to them. This disconnect between what we say we value and how we actually live creates a quiet but pervasive sense of unease—a feeling that we're not quite living our best lives.

When you're not living in alignment with your values, it shows up in your energy levels, your relationships, your work, and your overall sense of peace. — The Cost of Misalignment

Why Values-Based Living Changes Everything

In today's fast-paced, chaotic world, it's easy to get swept up in the demands of work, family, and social expectations. But when your values are unclear, your decisions become reactive, driven by external pressures rather than internal guidance.

This leads to a life that feels fragmented, unfulfilling, and ultimately unsustainable. You might experience increased stress, anxiety, and a nagging feeling that something is missing. And no amount of hustle or external achievement can truly fix a life that's fundamentally out of congruence.

The Hidden Cost

Living out of alignment with your values doesn't just affect your mood—it impacts your energy, relationships, work performance, and overall peace of mind.

Discover Your Core Values: Free Training Inside

If you're ready to bridge that gap and start living a life that truly reflects your deepest values, I have good news. I'm hosting a live training designed to help you get crystal clear on what you truly value and learn practical strategies for living in congruence with those values, every single day.

This isn't about empty affirmations or fleeting moments of motivation. This is about building a solid foundation for a life of purpose, meaning, and lasting satisfaction.

What You'll Learn in This Interactive Training

In this comprehensive training, we'll explore the crucial elements of values-based living. I'll guide you through a proven framework to help you identify your core values and translate them into actionable steps.

The Complete Framework We'll Cover:

1

The 5 Major Areas of Life

We'll examine health, relationships, career/mission, emotional well-being, and lifestyle, recognizing that values manifest differently in each domain.

2

Identifying Your Core Values in Each Area

Through guided exercises and thoughtful prompts, you'll uncover what truly matters most to you within each of the five key areas of life.

3

Defining What Values-Based Living Looks Like for You

We'll move beyond abstract concepts and get specific. What does "health" actually look like in your daily life? What does "connection" mean in your relationships? You'll create a clear and personalized vision.

4

Creating Practical Strategies for Alignment

You'll learn practical strategies for making decisions that align with your values and overcoming common obstacles. We'll explore time management techniques, boundary-setting strategies, and methods for prioritizing what truly matters.

5

Staying on Track When Life Gets Challenging

Life throws curveballs. You'll learn how to stay grounded in your values even when faced with challenges, setbacks, and unexpected changes.

What You'll Walk Away With

This training isn't just about learning concepts—it's about transformation. Here's what you'll gain:

Immediate Clarity

  • Unshakeable Clarity: A clear understanding of your core values across all major areas of your life
  • Increased Self-Trust: Confidence in your ability to make authentic decisions

Long-term Success

  • A Practical Framework: A tangible, actionable plan for integrating your values into daily life
  • Renewed Purpose: A deeper connection to your "why" and powerful sense of direction

Coach's Insight

The most successful people I work with aren't necessarily the most talented—they're the ones who make decisions from a place of clear values rather than external pressure.

Stop Drifting, Start Designing Your Life

It's time to stop drifting and start designing a life that truly reflects who you are and what matters most to you. It's time to step into your power and live with intention, purpose, and joy.

When you align your daily actions with your deepest values, everything changes. Your energy increases, your relationships improve, your work becomes more fulfilling, and you experience a profound sense of peace and authenticity.

Key Takeaways

  • Most people drift through life because they haven't clearly defined their core values
  • Living out of alignment creates stress, anxiety, and a persistent feeling that something is missing
  • Values-based living requires both clarity and practical strategies for daily implementation
  • True fulfillment comes from aligning your actions with your deepest values across all areas of life
