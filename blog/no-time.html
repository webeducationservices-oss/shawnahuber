No Time

Shawn Huber · April 13, 2023

Running a business is hard work. There are a million things to do, and it often seems like there's never enough time in the day to get everything done. When you're constantly feeling rushed and overwhelmed, frustration can quickly set in. But fear not! I've got a hilarious story to share that will help you overcome your frustration and reclaim control of your time.

Once upon a time, there was a young entrepreneur named Sam. Sam had a dream of running a successful business, but he quickly found himself drowning in a sea of tasks and responsibilities. He spent all day, every day, running from one meeting to the next, answering phone calls and emails, and trying to keep up with the never-ending stream of work that seemed to pile up on his desk. One day, Sam hit his breaking point. He was so overwhelmed that he couldn't even think straight. He stormed into his office, slammed the door shut, and began pacing back and forth. "This is impossible!" he shouted to no one in particular. "There's just not enough time in the day!"
