Tips For Helping Lessen Anxiety

Shawn Huber · March 1, 2023

Anxiety touches millions of lives, manifesting as persistent worry, overwhelming fear, and physical tension that can derail your daily routine and overall well-being. The good news? You don't have to let anxiety control your life.

While anxiety feels overwhelming in the moment, there are proven techniques you can use right now to calm your nervous system and regain control. Let's explore practical strategies that can help you manage anxiety symptoms and build long-term mental resilience.

Understanding the Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT)

Tapping, also known as Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT), is a powerful tool that combines ancient acupressure principles with modern psychology. This technique involves using your fingertips to tap on specific meridian points on your body while focusing on the anxiety or negative emotion you're experiencing.

The beauty of EFT lies in its ability to calm your body's stress response in real-time. When you tap on these energy points while acknowledging your anxiety, you're essentially rewiring your nervous system's reaction to stress triggers.

Coach's Insight

The tapping process can be customized to target different types of anxiety - whether you're dealing with social anxiety, panic attacks, or general worry. The key is consistency and being specific about what you're feeling.

What makes tapping so effective is its accessibility. You can use this technique anywhere, anytime - whether you're in a meeting, stuck in traffic, or lying in bed with racing thoughts. It requires no special equipment, just your fingertips and a few minutes of focused attention.

The Choice Between Struggle and Systems

Here's the reality: you're going to face challenging moments in life. The question isn't whether difficulties will come - it's whether you'll be prepared for them. Do you want to go through life reactive and overwhelmed, or would you rather have a system and plan in place to create the best life possible?

It's all about choice. What are you going to choose? — Your Mental Framework Matters

Every day, you're making a choice between two paths: the path of reaction, where anxiety controls your decisions and limits your potential, or the path of preparation, where you've built mental systems that serve you when life gets tough.

The most successful people don't have fewer problems - they have better systems for handling them. They've made the choice to prioritize their mental health and build resilience before they need it most.

Building Your Support Network

Doing life with other like-minded people is much more enjoyable - and it's also crucial for managing anxiety effectively. Isolation feeds anxiety, while connection starves it.

When you surround yourself with people who understand the importance of mental health and personal growth, several things happen:

  • You normalize conversations about anxiety and mental wellness
  • You gain access to different coping strategies and perspectives
  • You create accountability for maintaining healthy habits
  • You build a safety net of support for challenging times

Consider joining groups, communities, or working with professionals who share your commitment to mental wellness. The investment in these relationships pays dividends when anxiety tries to convince you that you're alone in your struggles.

Key Takeaways

  • EFT tapping can be done anywhere, anytime to calm your stress response in the moment
  • You have a choice between living reactively or building systems for mental resilience
  • Surrounding yourself with like-minded, growth-oriented people amplifies your ability to manage anxiety
  • True fulfillment comes from prioritizing your mental health and meaningful relationships
