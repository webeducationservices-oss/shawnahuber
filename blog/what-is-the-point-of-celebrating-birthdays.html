What Is The Point Of Celebrating Birthdays

Shawn Huber · December 05, 2022

Birthdays are special days because they are the only days that remember and acknowledge the life of a person who has been born. They remind us that we are alive and have another year to live. Birthdays also remind us of the past and give us optimism for the future, allowing us to feel special on our day to celebrate.

Some people look forward to their Birth Days while others do not. Why is there such an extreme? Some people celebrate the entire month while others barely think about it. Interesting. So Today Is My Birthday and I want to share it with you. Why you might ask. It's because if you are reading this we have met in some way or another. There is a reason for this meeting and it is up to us to explore it. I may be able to share something, make an introduction, share a story or you will with me. If we do not explore this then nothing will come from it.

I am curious to connect and make both of our lives better from it. It may be small or it could be huge. The only way we will know is if it happens. Thanks for connecting and I am looking forward to seeing what we can do with this connection. Life is much more fun when you do it with people you know. Enjoy life and smile while doing it.
