Why Is Taking Action Important

Shawn Huber · October 12, 2022

To take action means to do something to get a particular result. It’s not enough to just think about what you could do – you have to actually do it.

You can start by asking yourself: What is it I really want? And then, what am I willing to do to get that? You can also ask yourself what are the first steps I need to take to move in that direction? Action can be hard to take, but that’s only due to the fact of misrepresentation of the possibilities. We often focus on how daunting our tasks are and forget that they can be broken down into smaller chunks that are much easier to tackle.

Action Allows You to Shift or Pivot Sometimes we get stuck in our ways and feel like there is no way out of our situation. But taking action allows us to shift from one path or direction into another. When we stop moving forward, we become stagnant and stuck in old patterns that no longer serve us well. Action Ignites Motivation The truth is that there are multiple ways through which you can achieve your goals and dreams. If you stay still and don’t take action, you will never know what these ways are. When we take action, we are creating momentum and inspiration for ourselves and others around us as well as showing ourselves that we can indeed make change happen! Motivation follows action like water flows downhill so when you move mountains others will follow suit! When you are stuck on something and feel like everything is falling apart, it’s easy for your emotions to take over and paralyze you from moving forward. But if you just take action and start moving again, even if it's just a little bit at first, then it will become easier every time after that until eventually all those emotions go away completely while you continue moving forward with your life without them affecting your progress anymore at all! Action Shrinks Failures Taking action shrinks failures by showing them for what they really are: learning experiences that allowed us to grow and evolve into better versions of ourselves!
