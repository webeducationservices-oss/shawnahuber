Working On Yourself To Grow A Better Life And Business

Shawn Huber · August 23, 2022

Working on yourself isn't just about personal development—it's the foundation of everything else in your life, including your business success. When you invest in your physical health, mental clarity, and daily systems, you create a ripple effect that transforms every area of your performance.

The connection between personal growth and business growth isn't coincidental. Your business can only grow as much as you do. Here's how focusing on yourself creates the foundation for everything else to flourish.

The Four Pillars of Personal Performance

These foundational elements will transform both your life and your business when implemented consistently:

1

Better Eating Habits

Investing in proper nutrition isn't just about looking good—it's about healing your body and maximizing your energy. When you fuel yourself properly, you have the sustained energy needed for high-performance thinking and decision-making.

2

Understanding Sleep

Quality sleep is your secret weapon for recovery and mental clarity. When you master your sleep, you wake up with a sharper mind, better emotional regulation, and the cognitive capacity to tackle complex challenges.

3

Daily Routine for Stress Management

A consistent daily routine eliminates decision fatigue and reduces stress. When your day has structure, you free up mental energy for what matters most—growing your business and serving others.

4

Better Mental Communication

Improving how you communicate with yourself mentally—your internal dialogue, self-talk, and mindset patterns—directly impacts your confidence, decision-making speed, and ability to take action.

Key Insight

Creating a daily routine to balance sleep, exercise and relaxation will nourish your mind to make better decisions, take action faster, and think more clearly.

The Power of Simple Small Steps

If all you do is wish for something big—like a million dollars or a house on a tropical island—it might take forever before that wish comes true. But when you take small steps each day towards achieving those goals, they happen much sooner than expected.

Every day when you wake up, try this simple practice: write down three things you're grateful for, then plan your day accordingly. As you move through your day, keep this gratitude in mind so you're sending out positive energy consistently.

Small daily steps compound into extraordinary results faster than you expect. — The Compound Effect

Taking Time to Recharge Gives You More

There are times when everything feels like it's getting on top of you. But taking time out for yourself every day can make all those overwhelming tasks seem much less daunting when you return to them.

We all need to recharge our batteries, but many of us don't know how or when to do it effectively. Here's what works:

  • Schedule daily time where no phone or computer is around
  • Rest your mind and body for at least a few minutes before returning to work mode
  • Use this time for genuine mental and physical recovery

Coach's Insight

You'll be surprised how much more productive you become when you prioritize recovery. Rest isn't the opposite of productivity—it's what makes peak performance possible.

Your Business Grows When You Do

When you work on yourself to be healthier and more productive, you're doing the best thing possible for your business growth. These improvements in your personal foundation create:

  • Better decision-making from improved mental clarity
  • Faster action-taking from increased energy and confidence
  • Clearer thinking from proper rest and nutrition
  • Reduced stress from consistent daily routines

Key Takeaways

  • Your business can only grow as much as you do—personal development drives professional success
  • Focus on the four pillars: nutrition, sleep, routine, and mental communication
  • Take small daily steps rather than waiting for big breakthroughs
  • Regular recharge time increases your overall productivity and performance
