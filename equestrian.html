For Equestrian Athletes

Mental Wellness for Equestrian Athletes

You have the talent, the training, and the hours in the saddle. What's holding you back isn't your riding — it's what happens in your head at the gate. We help riders ride calm, confident, and connected, because the horse feels everything you feel.

Sound Familiar?

What's Really Holding You Back in the Saddle

You freeze up at shows despite being prepared +

You've put in the hours, you know your patterns, your horse is ready. But when it's time to enter the ring, something takes over — nerves, self-doubt, overthinking. Your body tenses and your horse feels every bit of it.

Fear and hesitation linger after a fall or injury +

One rough moment and suddenly you're questioning everything. The fear creeps in, your trust wavers, and rides that used to feel natural now feel heavy with anxiety. Getting back to where you were feels impossible.

You ride great at home but fall apart at the show +

In schooling you're smooth, relaxed, and dialed in. Then the show ring comes and it's a different rider on board. The inconsistency is maddening — you know you can do it, so why won't it show up when it counts?

You overthink everything at the gate +

The course is memorized, the plan is set — then your mind starts racing. Second-guessing, replaying mistakes, bracing for what could go wrong. By the time you pick up the canter, you're already in your head instead of in the ride.

The pressure from trainers and parents weighs on you +

Everyone has expectations, and you feel them every time you compete. The fear of letting people down crowds out the joy of riding, and you start performing for approval instead of for yourself and your horse.

Your confidence dips and you can't shake it +

A few bad outings and the doubt sets in. You hesitate where you used to commit, you play it safe, and the partnership that once felt effortless now feels like a constant battle of wills. You want to feel sure of yourself again.

What Changes

How Riders Show Up Differently

Composure at the Gate

You walk to the in-gate steady and clear instead of rattled. The nerves don't take the reins — you do.

Confidence in the Saddle

You commit to the distance, trust your training, and ride forward instead of second-guessing every stride.

Bounce Back After a Bad Ride

One mistake or fall no longer derails your season. You reset, recover mentally, and get back to riding like yourself.

Consistency from Schooling to Show

The rider you are at home is the rider who shows up in the ring — no more falling apart when it counts.

Calm the Horse Can Feel

When you settle, your horse settles. A calm rider creates a calm ride, and the partnership comes back together.

Enjoying Riding Again

You remember why you started. The pressure lifts, the joy returns, and riding feels like something you love again.

Coaching, not therapy

The Mental Mechanics provides mental wellness coaching focused on performance, confidence, and mindset in the saddle. This is not therapy or mental-health treatment. If you're dealing with a clinical condition, we'll always encourage you to work with a licensed professional — and coaching can be a great complement alongside that care.

Rachael Winograd is a celebrated equestrian, renowned for her mastery in saddle seat and hunt seat equitation, culminating in her earning the title of People's Choice Junior Exhibitor Of The Year in 2021.
