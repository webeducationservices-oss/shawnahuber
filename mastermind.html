Group or private — not sure which fits?

Group sessions give you community, accountability, and peer perspective. Private (1-on-1) sessions go deeper on your specific goals. Many high achievers do both. Reach out and we'll help you choose — or explore how coaching works.

Coaching, not therapy

The Mental Mechanics offers mental wellness coaching focused on mindset, performance, and growth. It is not therapy, counseling, or a substitute for mental health treatment. If you're dealing with a clinical or crisis concern, please reach out to a licensed professional.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a sales pitch? +

No. Group sessions are real coaching working sessions focused on your mental game and results. Your first time in, you're welcome to listen and absorb or jump in — entirely your call.

How is group coaching different from private sessions? +

Group sessions give you community, peer accountability, and perspective from other high achievers working the same inner game. Private 1-on-1 sessions go deeper on your specific goals. Many people do both — reach out and we'll help you pick.

How long is each group session? +

Most group sessions run about 60–75 minutes, live via Zoom — Thursdays at 6:00 PM ET and Fridays at 12:00 PM ET.

What will we work on? +

The mental side of high performance — mindset shifts, handling pressure, accountability, and breaking the patterns that hold driven people back. Each session works real challenges the group brings, so you leave with practical tools and momentum.

Is this therapy? +

No. This is mental wellness coaching for performance and growth, led by Shawn Huber. It's not therapy or a substitute for mental health treatment. If you need clinical support, please connect with a licensed professional.
