12-Session System

Soccer Mindset Mastery System

Get ready to revolutionize the way you approach your soccer journey. A 12-session system merging personal development and soccer skills in a unique, impactful way.

Sound Familiar?

What’s Happening Off the Pitch That’s Showing On It

You lose composure after a missed opportunity or bad call +

A missed shot on goal, a referee’s bad call, and your head drops. Frustration takes over and suddenly you’re playing angry instead of playing smart. The rest of the match suffers because you can’t let it go.

You’re playing it safe instead of taking creative risks +

You used to try things — skill moves, through balls, runs into space. Now you play the safe pass every time because you’re afraid of making mistakes. The creative player you used to be is hiding behind fear.

Team dynamics are dragging you down mentally +

Maybe it’s a coach who doesn’t believe in you, teammates who are negative, or the politics of playing time. The environment around you feels toxic and it’s seeping into your game and your love for soccer.

The pressure from parents, coaches, and scouts is crushing you +

Everyone has expectations — your family, your coaches, the scouts on the sideline. The weight of performing for everyone else has made you forget why you started playing in the first place.

Course Overview

Revolutionize Your Soccer Journey

This 12-session system is designed specifically for soccer players, exploring self-awareness, consistency and change, and accountability. You’ll develop the mental tools to perform at your best on and off the pitch, fusing personal growth with soccer performance in a distinct and powerful manner.

Block 1 — Sessions 1–4

Building Self-Awareness

Session 1: Comprehensive Personal Assessment

Dive deep into your soccer goals and aspirations through a Personal Assessment & Evaluation.

Session 2: Certainty

Understand the balance between certainty and uncertainty, and how your beliefs shape your soccer experience on and off the field.

Session 3: Energy Checklist

Enhance your energy and vibrancy. Improve your health and performance on the pitch.

Session 4: Confidence

Explore how past experiences have shaped your confidence. Learn when to step up and when to hold back.

Guided Visualization Session

A powerful session synthesizing insights from the previous four sessions, ingraining them into your subconscious mind.

Block 2 — Sessions 5–8

Consistency and Change

Session 5: Finding Efficiencies & Effectiveness

Streamline your productivity, balance routines, and discover new ways to perform on the soccer field.

Session 6: Sway & Alignment

Master the art of influence and communication in your relationships and soccer career.

Session 7: Mastering Your Mind

Convert previous lessons into actionable strategies aligned with your soccer goals.

Session 8: Physiology

Understand the connection between your activities, habits, physical well-being, and soccer performance.

Guided Visualization Session

A powerful session synthesizing insights from the recent four sessions, ingraining them into your subconscious mind.

Block 3 — Sessions 9–12

Accountability & Success

Sessions 9 through 12 are dedicated accountability sessions. This is where you solidify the changes you’ve made, develop a deep sense of responsibility toward your efficiencies, effectiveness, sway, alignment, mind mastery, and physiology.

It’s time to take control of your mindset and unlock your full potential as a soccer player.
