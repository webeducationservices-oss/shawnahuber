About the Speaker

The Journey and Vision of Shawn Huber

Shawn Huber, known as The Mental Mechanic, is an author, speaker, and Certified High Performance Coach accredited by the prestigious High Performance Institute, and a Certified Hypnotherapist.

As the visionary founder of Structured Freedom, a cutting-edge Transformational Coaching enterprise, Shawn leads individuals on a profound journey to harmonize mind and body, catalyzing a transformation towards vitality and well-being.

Boasting a fifteen-year coaching career, Shawn's transformative touch has empowered a diverse clientele, all united by the pursuit of growth from "Good to Great."

Shawn's spirit for realizing human potential is forged from his own life-altering trials. In a pivotal moment in 2003, Shawn was diagnosed with cancer. His response: "Things happen. What's next?" This unshakeable attitude has been his beacon throughout his entire life.

Shawn lives in Florida with his wife, Megan and daughter, Brighton.
