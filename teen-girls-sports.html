12-Session System

Teen Girls in Sports Mindset Mastery System

Empower your game both on and off the field. A 12-session system designed specifically for teen girls in individual and team sports, merging personal development with athletic excellence and building the confidence to lead.

Sound Familiar?

What’s Happening Off the Field That’s Showing On It

You compare yourself to other girls in your sport +

Whether you’re on a team or competing individually, you see other girls succeeding and suddenly feel not good enough. Comparison steals your joy and makes you doubt your abilities.

You’re struggling with confidence and self-worth +

Off the field, you feel insecure about who you are. Maybe it’s your body, your abilities, or your place on the team. These feelings seep into your performance and hold you back from being the athlete you could be.

Pressure from parents, coaches, and peers is overwhelming +

Everyone has expectations and opinions about your athletic future. The weight of trying to please everyone has made you lose sight of what YOU actually want from sports and your life.

You struggle with perfectionism and fear of failure +

One mistake in practice and you spiral. You’re hard on yourself, afraid to take risks, and missing out on the growth and enjoyment that comes from pushing your limits and learning from setbacks.

Course Overview

Unlock Your Full Potential

This 12-session system is designed specifically for teen girls in both individual and team sports, exploring self-awareness, consistency and change, and accountability. You’ll develop the mental tools to perform at your best on and off the field while building the resilience and confidence to lead—in sports and in life.

Block 1 — Sessions 1–4

Building Self-Awareness

Session 1: Personal Assessment

Dive deep into your athletic goals, dreams, and who you want to become. Whether you’re pursuing individual accolades or team success, define what matters most to you.

Session 2: Certainty

Discover your core beliefs about your abilities. Learn how self-doubt shows up in your game and how to shift it.

Session 3: Energy Checklist

Boost your physical and mental energy. Better sleep, nutrition, and mindset mean better athletic performance.

Session 4: Confidence

Build unshakeable confidence. Explore where your confidence comes from and how to access it when you need it most.

Guided Visualization Session

A powerful session synthesizing insights from the previous four sessions, ingraining them into your subconscious mind.

Block 2 — Sessions 5–8

Consistency and Change

Session 5: Finding Efficiencies & Effectiveness

Streamline your daily routine. Balance school, sports, and social life without burning out.

Session 6: Influence & Communication

Lead with confidence and stand up for yourself. Learn to communicate authentically—whether you’re leading teammates or inspiring those around you as an individual athlete.

Session 7: Mastering Your Mind

Turn mental challenges into mental strength. Control self-talk, manage pressure, and perform under stress.

Session 8: Physiology & Wellness

Understand how your body and mind work together. Build healthy habits that fuel athletic performance.

Guided Visualization Session

A powerful session synthesizing insights from the recent four sessions, ingraining them into your subconscious mind.

Block 3 — Sessions 9–12

Accountability & Success

Sessions 9 through 12 are dedicated accountability sessions. This is where you solidify the changes you’ve made and develop a deep sense of responsibility toward your goals, your confidence, your leadership, and your success.

It’s time to take control of your mindset, lead with confidence, and unlock your full potential as an athlete and a young woman.
